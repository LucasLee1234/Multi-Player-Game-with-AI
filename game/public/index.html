How to play
  1. Your route shows your own Safe, Danger, and Unknown cells. You can see the partner's full danger layer.
  2. Select a cell on the partner's map to send one truthful signal per turn. Each robot has exactly two hazards; learning both lets you deduce other cells are safe.
  3. Propose an adjacent move or Wait. Review both proposals, then both press Ready. Changed plans or signals clear both confirmations.
  4. Both waiting uses a turn. Overlap and direct swaps block movement; following into a vacated cell is allowed. Hazards add strikes before collisions resolve.
  5. Finish together within eight turns and fewer than three strikes. Unknown is not Safe; hazards differ between robots.
How to play
  1. You control your own robot. Your partner controls the other one.
  2. Stand on a relay to power its linked gate. Select a relay or gate to inspect the link shown under the map.
  3. Click a direction or use arrow keys / WASD to move immediately. Your partner moves independently. Do nothing to wait.
  4. Latching gates stay open after first entry. Hold-open gates need a robot to stay on their relay. A gate closing never traps a robot already inside; that robot can leave.
  5. Bring both robots to their labeled exits together. You can leave an exit to help. Use spare spaces to let your partner pass; occupied tiles block movement. There is no move limit.
  6. Select a floor tile to point it out to your partner. Standing on a relay powers its gate immediately.
